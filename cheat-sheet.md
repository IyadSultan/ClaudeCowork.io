  <h2>Five rules</h2>
  <ol class="cw-steps">
    <li>One dedicated folder. Synthetic, public or de-identified files only.</li>
    <li>Manual mode until a task has worked three times.</li>
    <li>Ask for the plan first.</li>
    <li>Check every file before it leaves your desk.</li>
    <li>Never point Cowork at a clinical system or a patient record.</li>
  </ol>
