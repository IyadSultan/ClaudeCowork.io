  <h2>Six ideas worth keeping</h2>
  <ol class="cw-steps">
    <li><strong>Outcome first, not task first.</strong> Chat gets step-by-step instructions. Cowork gets the end result, the constraints and the quality bar: &ldquo;I have 15 photos in this folder. I need them in subfolders by topic with descriptive names.&rdquo;</li>
    <li><strong>Start with guardrails.</strong> His starter instructions say: before deleting, overwriting or renaming any file, show me what will change and wait. Training wheels you can remove later. Our Block 1 instructions do the same.</li>
    <li><strong>One playground folder.</strong> Make <code>Documents/Cowork Playground</code> and work only there. A file dragged in from elsewhere may load but not be readable.</li>
    <li><strong>Memory lives in files.</strong> Tell Cowork to save your preferences to <code>CLAUDE.md</code> and <code>memory.md</code> in the folder root. Every correction you make there improves the next run.</li>
    <li><strong>Do the job first, then make the skill.</strong> Work through the task with Cowork, give feedback until the output is right, then say &ldquo;turn this whole workflow into a skill.&rdquo; Back up your skills; they do not move to a new computer on their own.</li>
    <li><strong>Schedules work because of the rest.</strong> His 6 a.m. inbox triage works because the rules sit in a Markdown file, Gmail is connected, and weeks of corrections sit in memory.</li>
  </ol>

  <div class="callout callout-note">
    Two cautions from the video. He finds the Claude browser extension slow, unreliable and heavy on usage, and notes that Cowork cannot be forced to search the web the way Chat can. These are his April 2026 findings; Anthropic has since added a built-in browser (Block 3).
  </div>

  <div class="callout callout-safety">
    His inbox and Gmail tone-of-voice examples read real email. At KHCC, connect only accounts and folders that hold no patient information, and keep scheduled tasks to drafts you review, never automatic sends.
